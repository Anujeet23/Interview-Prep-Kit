About Acme Ledger

Acme Ledger was founded in 2019 by two former payments engineers who were tired of rebuilding ledgers at every company. Our mission is to make money movement auditable by default.

Our values

  • Correctness over cleverness
  • Write it down: we are a documentation-first, async company
  • Customers' money is sacred

Our customers include marketplaces, neobanks and B2B payment platforms.
