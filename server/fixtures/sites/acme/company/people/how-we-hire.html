How we hire engineers

Our interview process has four stages and usually takes two weeks.

  1. Recruiter call: 30 minutes on your background and what you are looking for.
  2. Take-home exercise: a small ledger service you build in your own time (about 3 hours). We pay candidates for their time.
  3. Technical interview: 60 minutes walking through your take-home with two engineers and extending it together.
  4. System design interview: 60 minutes designing a money-movement system at scale.
  5. Values interview: a behavioural conversation with a founder about how you work.

We do not ask algorithm puzzles or whiteboard trivia.
